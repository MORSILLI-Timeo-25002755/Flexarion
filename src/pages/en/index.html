01 / The studio

An independent studio driven by creative craft

Flexarion is an independent game development studio based in Aix-en-Provence, southern France. We design and engineer interactive worlds where art, technology, and playfulness meet.

Built to protect editorial freedom, our studio brings together game design, art direction, and software engineering to craft memorable indie games for players worldwide.

Explore Flexarion’s vision and method

01 — INDEPENDENCE

Creative freedom

An independent structure that puts artistic intent, world coherence, and gameplay feel ahead of standardized formulas.

02 — CRAFT

Technical & artistic mastery

From early mechanics prototyping to sound and lighting direction, every layer of game development is tailored to the experience.

03 — AMBITION

Local roots, global reach

Rooted in Aix-en-Provence’s creative ecosystem with the ambition to share our games with players across the globe.
