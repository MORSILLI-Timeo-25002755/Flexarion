Personal data protection — GDPR PRIVACY & DATA

1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data collected through this site is Flexarion (Aix-en-Provence, France).

2. Data Collected

We apply strict data minimization. The only information potentially collected is what you voluntarily provide when contacting us (via the form or email): name or handle, email address, subject, and message content.

By default, this site is static: until a third-party form backend is connected, no form data is transmitted to a server.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis

Contact details are used solely to read and reply to your inquiry (legitimate interest or pre-contractual steps). Your data is never sold, shared, or used for unsolicited marketing.

4. Retention

Correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and for up to 24 months after the last contact unless required otherwise by law.

5. Cookies & Trackers

This website uses no advertising cookies, no behavioral trackers, and no third-party analytics. Only an ephemeral sessionStorage flag is used during navigation to animate page transitions, without any personal data or cross-site tracking.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

7. Contact

To exercise your rights, write to contact@flexarion.timeoms.fr.
