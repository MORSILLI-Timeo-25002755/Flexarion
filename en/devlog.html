LOG #03 FLX-01 · Les Veilleurs de Brume November 16, 2026

Les Veilleurs de Brume: Prototyping core mechanics & gameplay loop

Before locking down environments or final art assets, our current tests on Les Veilleurs de Brume focus purely on immediate feel on controller and keyboard: input responsiveness, movement clarity, and interaction pacing around the sunken city’s beacons.

Working in greybox lets us iterate quickly, test bold design hypotheses, and keep only what truly elevates the player experience.

LOG #02 Art Direction November 1, 2026

Light, contrast, and Flexarion’s visual grammar

Our visual research starts from a clear rule: silhouette readability and gameplay clarity come before visual clutter. We explore dark, high-contrast atmospheres punctuated by sharp light accents to guide the player’s eye naturally.
